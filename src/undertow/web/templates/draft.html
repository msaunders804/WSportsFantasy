Welcome to the draft

A draft is how everyone builds their team, taking turns choosing players. You'll go fourth. The computer drafts for the other nine teams.

  1. Snake order. Round 1 goes 1 to 10. Round 2 reverses, 10 back to 1, so the team that picks last also picks first in the next round. {{ help("Fairer than the same order every round. You pick 4th in round 1, then 17th overall, because round 2 runs backwards.") }}
  2. Build a balanced team. You need 4 forwards (one is the flex UTIL spot), 2 defenders and 1 goalie to start, plus 4 on the bench. {{ help("Starters score points for you each week. Bench players score nothing, but they cover injuries and byes.") }}
  3. Not sure? Tap Recommended. We suggest a pick and explain why. Turn on auto-draft and we'll pick for you. {{ help("Auto-draft takes the top player in your queue first. If your queue is empty, it uses our recommendation. It also kicks in if your clock runs out.") }}
