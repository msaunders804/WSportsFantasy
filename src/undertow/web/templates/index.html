How it works

No spreadsheets, no jargon. Here's the whole season in three steps.

  1. Join a league

    Two leagues of 10 teams each. You'll get an invite link and set up your team in about a minute.

  2. Draft your team

    A live snake draft with a pick timer and tips along the way. Never drafted before? We'll walk you through it.

  3. Play head-to-head

    Each week your players' real PWHL games score points against another team in your league. The numbers move, and so do the stories.

Questions

I've never played fantasy. Is this for me?

Yes, that's who we built it for. We explain each step as it comes up, and you don't need to know hockey stats to start.

Is it free?

Yes. It's free to join and play.

No. Undertow is an independent fan project with no affiliation with the PWHL or its teams.
