I'm the face behind Undertow Sports. I grew up playing sports and I'll get behind any women's sports team or league — so when the PWHL kicked off its inaugural season, I was in from day one.

Last year I started digging into the numbers, posting PWHL stats and analysis as Undertow Sports on TikTok. Right now, it's just me building this.
